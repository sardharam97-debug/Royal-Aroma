Our Story

A vegetarian house built on patience, spice and hospitality

About Royal Aroma

Royal Aroma is a luxury vegetarian restaurant created for guests who want Indian dining to feel ceremonial. The kitchen draws from hotel kitchens of Ahmedabad, Delhi and Amritsar, then plates each course with a quieter, modern elegance.

Restaurant Story

The house began as a family table that welcomed relatives with Gujarati kadhi, Punjabi dal and a tray of sweets. Those recipes were later refined with slower reductions, better produce and a dining room dressed in black, teak and gold.

Luxury Dining

Low lighting, linen, brass service ware and a paced menu designed for long conversations.

Vegetarian Philosophy

We cook only vegetarian food. No shortcuts, no mixed kitchen, and a drinks list that stays family-friendly.

Quality Ingredients

Hand-pounded spices, seasonal vegetables, fresh dairy and breads rolled throughout service.

Professional Service

Hosts greet every table, captains explain the menu without rush, and the kitchen times courses so families, couples and celebrations all feel looked after. Whether you dine in or order from our website, the same kitchen prepares your meal.
